# Favicon Design Requirements: zimmy.dev

## 1. Overview
- **Target**: `zimmy.dev` blog
- **Main Topics**: Development stories in the era of AI and Agents.

## 2. Core Concept & Vibe
- **Design Philosophy (Contrast)**: While the topics (AI, Agents) are highly logical and technical, the visual representation should be the opposite.
- **Mood Keywords**: Warm (따뜻한), Handwritten (사람 글씨 같은), Calm (차분한), Hip (힙한), Creative (창의적인).
- **Style**: Abstract (추상형).

## 3. Selected Theme: "The Organic Node"
- **Form**: Hand-drawn, loose, and slightly imperfect circular loops or overlapping rings. It represents the connection of agents and nodes, but through a warm, human lens.
- **Key Visual Elements**: Organic, fluid lines instead of rigid geometric shapes.

## 4. Color Palette
- **Primary Color**: `Nano Banana` (A soft, calming creamy pastel yellow, ≈ `#FDEEAF`).
- **Accent/Stroke Colors**: Warm Charcoal or soft oatmeal tones to emphasize the handwritten, natural texture without the harshness of pure black.

## 5. Summary
A minimalist, abstract design that visually captures the connection between cold AI technology and warm human creativity. Clean, simple, and modern with a distinct "nano banana" color point.
