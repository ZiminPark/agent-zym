# OG Image Design Requirements: zimmy.dev (Main)

## 1. Overview
- **Target**: `zimmy.dev` blog main Open Graph (OG) image / Thumbnail.
- **Main Topics**: Development stories in the era of AI and Agents.
- **Purpose**: Represents the blog when the home link is shared on social media, messengers, etc.

## 2. Core Concept & Vibe
- **Design Philosophy (Contrast)**: "The Original Tool". While topics (AI, Agents) are logical and tech-heavy, the visual representation is the most analog, fundamental tool of creation.
- **Mood Keywords**: Warm, Handwritten, Calm, Hip, Creative.
- **Style**: Abstract, empty space (여백의 미).

## 3. Selected Theme: "Abstract 'Classic Pen Nib or Pencil' (Continuous Line)"
- **Form**: A single continuous line art forming a fluid and organic classic fountain pen nib or a pencil. It avoids geometric perfection and looks gracefully drawn by a human hand.
- **Visual Composition**: Pure illustration surrounded by generous empty space. No text at all (완전한 여백의 미).
- **Key Visual Elements**: Ultra-minimalist, thick handwritten stroke (like a marker pen), single continuous curve.

## 4. Color Palette
- **Primary/Background Color**: Solid Off-White or soft oatmeal tone.
- **Stroke Color**: Warm Charcoal (`#333333` to `#4A4A4A`) to emphasize the hand-drawn, natural texture without the harshness of pure black.

## 5. Summary
An ultra-minimalist, single continuous line art illustration of a classic pen nib or pencil. It is completely abstract, fluid, and imperfect, placed thoughtfully in the center of an expansive off-white background with zero text. The high contrast between the cutting-edge topic of AI/Agents and the analog warmth of the illustration creates a unique, highly impactful visual presence.

## 6. Image Generation Prompt (for Gemini/Midjourney)
> Prompt: "Create an ultra-minimalist, single continuous line art illustration of a classic fountain pen nib (or a single wooden pencil). The drawing must be completely abstract, fluid, organic, and imperfect, as if it was drawn gracefully by a human hand. Avoid any rigid geometric perfection or 3D realism. The stroke should be exactly one continuous thick line, colored in warm charcoal (#333333) resembling a marker pen drawing. The background must be a completely solid, soft oatmeal off-white color.
>
> CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS:
>
> NO TEXT OR LETTERS AT ALL. MUST NOT CONTAIN ANY TYPOGRAPHY.
Maximize the negative space (empty space). The drawing should be placed completely alone in the center or slightly off-center, surrounded by vast, completely empty off-white background to emphasize a calm, quiet, and Zen-like minimalist aesthetic (--ar 1.91:1 horizontal canvas)."
