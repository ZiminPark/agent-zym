# Favicon Design Requirements: zimmy.dev

## 1. Overview
- **Target**: `zimmy.dev` blog
- **Main Topics**: Development stories in the era of AI and Agents.

## 2. Core Concept & Vibe
- **Design Philosophy (Contrast)**: While the topics (AI, Agents) are highly logical and technical, the visual representation should be the opposite.
- **Mood Keywords**: Warm (따뜻한), Handwritten (사람 글씨 같은), Calm (차분한), Hip (힙한), Creative (창의적인).
- **Style**: Abstract (추상형).

## 3. Selected Theme: "Abstract 'Z' (Continuous Line)"
- **Form**: A single continuous line art forming a fluid and organic letter 'Z'. It avoids geometric perfection and looks like it was drawn gracefully by a human hand, representing a warm and imperfect node.
- **Key Visual Elements**: Ultra-minimalist, thick handwritten stroke (like a marker pen), single continuous curve.

## 4. Color Palette
- **Primary/Background Color**: Solid Off-White or soft oatmeal tone.
- **Stroke Color**: Warm Charcoal (`#333333` to `#4A4A4A`) to emphasize the hand-drawn, natural texture without the harshness of pure black.
- *(Note: Pastel yellow has been explicitly excluded to focus purely on linear warmth and minimalism)*

## 5. Summary
An ultra-minimalist, single continuous line art design that forms a fluid, organic 'Z'. It completely abandons rigid technology-focused visuals, opting for a conceptual, quiet, and calming handwritten vibe.
